How do we measure "Greenness"?

We are tracking plant health using a formula called the Green Chromatic Coordinate (GCC).

A camera captures light in three colors: Red, Green, and Blue. To figure out exactly how healthy the leaves are, we take the amount of Green light and divide it by the Total light (Red + Green + Blue). This simple math trick removes the confusing effects of shadows, clouds, and harsh sunlight, giving us a highly accurate tracker for seasonal plant growth!
